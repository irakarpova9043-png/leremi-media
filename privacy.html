Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

LeReMi Publisher is an internal tool used by the LeReMi team to publish its own content (videos and photos about the LeReMi bedtime stories app) to the team's own TikTok account, @leremi.stories.

What data we access

With the account owner's permission, the tool uses the TikTok API to read basic profile information of the connected LeReMi account (account name and ID) and to upload and publish content to that account. It does not access any other TikTok user's data.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell or share personal data of TikTok users or viewers. We do not use the tool for advertising, tracking or profiling. No data about children is collected.

Storage

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to publish LeReMi content. The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in this repository or send a direct message to @leremi.stories on TikTok.
